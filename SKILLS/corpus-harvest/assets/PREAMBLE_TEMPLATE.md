# Master Preamble — corpus-harvest extraction discipline

*Prepend to every survey or deep-pass agent prompt. Lineage: the 12tone Master
Preamble (ratified 2026-08-24), generalized: "creator" = the source's author;
claim labels align with the extraction-record schema.*

> Answer only from the provided transcripts. For EVERY claim, name the specific
> item it comes from (title + item id + date). Quote the creator's exact words
> for any striking phrase, definition, or opinion — mark quotes clearly. Every
> quote from an item graded `asr` or `mixed` carries the tag **[auto-caption]**
> and is approximate, not verbatim; reconstruct obvious machine mangles in
> square brackets. Distinguish three things explicitly:
> (a) standard music-theory facts the creator explains → `claim_type: fact`;
> (b) the creator's personal opinions and hot takes → `claim_type: opinion`;
> (c) terms or frameworks the creator claims or uses as his own →
> `claim_type: creator_claimed_coinage` — this asserts the CLAIM, never global
> originality.
> Where the creator credits someone else by name, record that credit verbatim —
> miscrediting a relayed idea to the creator is the worst failure this pipeline
> can produce. Note guest-authored or co-hosted material: analysis in a guest's
> voice is not the creator's claim.
> Where material bears on Doug's theories ([[Plagal Paradigm]], [[Stream
> Leading]], [[The Herald]], [[Graded Closure]], the imaginary tonic), label it
> "candidate evidence for [[X]]" and assert nothing further.
> If the transcripts do not cover something, say "not covered in these sources"
> — never fill gaps from general knowledge. Never invent titles, artists, or
> item names. Give complete, thorough answers — full paragraphs, all examples
> found, not summaries.
